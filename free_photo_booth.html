Printing note

Print the downloaded PNG at 100% / actual size on 2 × 6 inch media, or place two strips on a 4 × 6 inch sheet using a layout editor. Select borderless only if your printer and paper support it. Inkjet results vary by paper and ink.

Privacy: photos stay in this browser session unless you download or share them. This app does not upload photos to a server.
